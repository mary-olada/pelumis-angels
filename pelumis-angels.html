Our Story

Food is Love
Made Visible

Pelumi's Angels was born from a deep belief that food is more than sustenance — it is ceremony, memory, and connection. Every dish we create carries intention, soul, and the warmth of hands that truly care.

From our curated menu and chef-crafted meal prep kits to our immersive cooking classes and intimate private dinners, we invite you into a world where flavour meets artistry — a place where every bite is a taste of paradise.

This is not just a food business. This is Pelumi's Angels — your all-in-one culinary sanctuary.

🌿

EBT Verification

We keep this simple, private, and secure. Upload a photo of your EBT/SNAP card (you may cover your card number — we only need to confirm the card type) and we'll unlock your discounted access within 24 hours.

1
Take a photo of your EBT/Lone Star card. You may cover all numbers except the first 4 digits for privacy.
2
Upload it below. We accept JPG, PNG, or PDF. Your information is never shared or stored beyond verification.
3
We'll email you your discounted access code within 24 hours. Verification is renewed annually.
📎
Click to upload your EBT card photo JPG · PNG · PDF · Max 5MB · Encrypted & secure
